Privacy policy

{{ site.operator }}{% if site.address %}, {{ site.address }},{% endif %} is the controller of your personal data on {{ site.name }}. Contact for anything about your data: {{ site.contact }}.

1. What we collect

  • Account: username, name, email address, a password stored only as a bcrypt hash (we cannot read it), your “about” text, whether you are a fan, band or venue, optional location and website, the date you joined and the date you accepted the terms.
  • What you post: posts, gig announcements, photos, comments, likes, and private messages you send.
  • Moderation: reports you file or that are filed about your content, blocks you set, and actions moderators take.
  • Security: a sign-in session (stored as a hash) and short-lived counters derived from your IP address and username that stop password guessing and spam. They are deleted after at most a day. Our web server may also keep ordinary access logs (time, address, page requested).

Gigs near you. Bands and venues can add the position of a gig. The place name they type may be looked up on the map with a geocoding service (by default OpenStreetMap's Nominatim; only that place text is sent, no member data). When you use Gigs near you, your browser asks your permission, and your approximate position (rounded to about 1 km) is sent to us only to find gigs: it is not stored, not logged and not shared. Some listings in that search come from event partners (Ticketmaster, and where we use them Skiddle and Songkick). To list gigs in a place nobody searched before, we ask them for events in the rounded area (a grid square of about 28 km) around the search; they never receive the exact position, your name or any account detail. Ticket links lead to the partner's own site, which has its own privacy policy. Bands may connect their own Bandsintown page.

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not load anything from third-party servers: the fonts and scripts of the site are served by us.

2. Why, and on what legal basis

  • To run your account and show your content to other members: performance of the contract (the terms).
  • To keep the community safe (reports, blocking, spam and abuse protection, security logs): legitimate interests.
  • To send the emails that the service needs (confirm your address, reset your password): performance of the contract. We do not send marketing emails.

3. Who can see it

Your profile, posts, photos and comments are visible to signed-in members of {{ site.name }} (your profile page is visible to anyone). Photos are not public: they are only served to signed-in members. Private messages are visible only to you and the person you write to, and to no one else, including moderators, unless you report a problem and send us the text.

4. How long we keep it

Until you delete it. When you delete your account we erase your profile, posts, photos, comments, likes and private conversations (a conversation is removed for both people, because it cannot exist with one side missing). Backups may keep copies for a short, limited time. Records of moderation actions are kept without your content.

5. Your rights

You can, at any time, from the Account page: download a copy of your data (access and portability), correct it (Edit profile), and delete your account (erasure). You can also ask us to restrict or stop using your data, or object to it, by email. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to your data protection authority (in Ireland, the Data Protection Commission, dataprotection.ie).

6. Service providers

We use providers to host the service, store photos and send email. They only process data for us and under our instructions. Ask {{ site.contact }} for the current list. Data is not sold. If a provider is outside the European Economic Area we rely on an appropriate safeguard such as standard contractual clauses.

7. Children

{{ site.name }} is for people aged {{ site.min_age }} and over. If you think a younger person has joined, tell us and we will delete the account.

8. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters to you we will tell you in the service or by email.
