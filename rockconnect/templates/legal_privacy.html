Privacy policy

{{ site.operator }}{% if site.address %}, {{ site.address }},{% endif %} is the controller of your personal data on {{ site.name }}. Contact for anything about your data: {{ site.contact }}.

1. What we collect

  • Account: username, name, email address, a password stored only as a bcrypt hash (we cannot read it), your “about” text, whether you are a fan, band or venue, optional location and website, the date you joined and the date you accepted the terms. We also ask for your date of birth, only to check that you are at least {{ site.min_age }}. It cannot be changed afterwards, is never shown to anyone, and is kept so the check holds.
  • What you post: posts, gig announcements, photos, comments, likes, and private messages you send. Messages you write under a gig (the gig discussion) are visible to the signed-in members who look at that gig.
  • Who you follow: the members you follow and the members who follow you, and requests that were not answered yet. Following a public account does not need their permission; following a private account needs them to accept. They get a notification in the app. You can stop or withdraw a request at any time.
  • Notifications: a short note when someone follows you or when a member you follow says they are going to a gig. You can switch the gig notices off in Edit profile. Read notices are deleted after 30 days, all of them after 90 days.
  • Your music and plans: the instruments you play, the genres you like, what you are looking for (for example gig buddies or bandmates), and the gigs you say you are going to or interested in. For a gig that was imported from an event service we keep a small copy (title, place, date) with your plan, and delete it a day after the gig.
  • Moderation: reports you file or that are filed about your content, blocks you set, and actions moderators take. To keep the community safe, a post, comment or gig comment may be checked before other members see it: automatically (a list of blocked words, extra checks on new accounts, and when several members report it) and then by a moderator, who sees the text and why it was held. Until the check is done only you can see it. A copy of the held text is kept in the review queue until a moderator decides, and is deleted with your account.
  • Security: a sign-in session (stored as a hash) and short-lived counters derived from your IP address and username that stop password guessing and spam. They are deleted after at most a day. Our web server may also keep ordinary access logs (time, address, page requested).

Gigs near you. Bands and venues can add the position of a gig. The place name they type may be looked up on the map with a geocoding service (by default OpenStreetMap's Nominatim; only that place text is sent, no member data). When you use Gigs near you, your browser asks your permission, and your approximate position (rounded to about 1 km) is sent to us only to find gigs: it is not stored, not logged and not shared. Some listings in that search come from event partners (Ticketmaster, and where we use them Skiddle, Songkick and PredictHQ). To list gigs in a place nobody searched before, we ask them for events in the rounded area (a grid square of about 28 km) around the search; they never receive the exact position, your name or any account detail. Ticket links lead to the partner's own site, which has its own privacy policy. Bands may connect their own Bandsintown page.

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not load anything from third-party servers: the fonts and scripts of the site are served by us.

2. Why, and on what legal basis

  • To run your account and show your content to other members: performance of the contract (the terms).
  • To keep the community safe (reports, blocking, spam and abuse protection, security logs): legitimate interests.
  • To send the emails that the service needs (confirm your address, reset your password): performance of the contract. We do not send marketing emails.

3. Who can see it

Your gig plans. Signed-in members can see which gigs you are going to and your instruments, genres and goals, so people can find each other. You control this: on any gig you can keep your name private (you are still counted, but not listed), and in Edit profile you can hide all your plans. You can make your account private in Edit profile: people can still find you and see your name, picture and bio, but your posts, photos, comments and plans are shown only to members you accept (a request to follow you waits for your answer). Members you follow are told when you say you are going, unless you hide your plans or keep that gig private. Members you block, and members who block you, never see them. Nobody can message you until you accept their message request; you can also decline or ignore it, and the request carries no text.

Your profile, posts, photos and comments are visible to signed-in members of {{ site.name }} (your profile page is visible to anyone). Photos are not public: they are only served to signed-in members. Private messages are visible only to you and the person you write to, and to no one else, including moderators, unless you report a problem and send us the text.

4. How long we keep it

A sign-up that has not been confirmed with the link we email is not an account: it is kept for at most 24 hours and then deleted.

Until you delete it. When you delete your account we erase your profile, date of birth, posts, photos, comments, gig plans, gig discussion messages, follows, notifications, likes and private conversations (a conversation is removed for both people, because it cannot exist with one side missing). Backups may keep copies for a short, limited time. Records of moderation actions are kept without your content.

5. Your rights

You can, at any time, from the Account page: download a copy of your data (access and portability), correct it (Edit profile), and delete your account (erasure). You can also ask us to restrict or stop using your data, or object to it, by email. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to your data protection authority (in Ireland, the Data Protection Commission, dataprotection.ie).

6. Service providers

We use providers to host the service, store photos and send email. They only process data for us and under our instructions. Ask {{ site.contact }} for the current list. Data is not sold. If a provider is outside the European Economic Area we rely on an appropriate safeguard such as standard contractual clauses.

7. Children

{{ site.name }} is for people aged {{ site.min_age }} and over. If you think a younger person has joined, tell us and we will delete the account. We check the date of birth given at sign-up, and an account whose date of birth shows the person is under {{ site.min_age }} is closed.

8. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters to you we will tell you in the service or by email.
